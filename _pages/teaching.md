## Current courses

### Social Research Methods

**Kuwait University · Fall 2026**

An introduction to designing and critically evaluating social research. Students develop a complete research proposal while learning how to formulate research questions, review literature, measure concepts, select samples, design data-collection instruments, and evaluate methodological evidence.
